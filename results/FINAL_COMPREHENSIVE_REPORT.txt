EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

This study examines anthropomorphization of AI companions among Reddit users,
with a focus on how demographics (age, gender) and usage intent relate to
anthropomorphization levels.

KEY FINDINGS:
1. AGE MATTERS: Teens show significantly higher peak anthropomorphization (p<0.001)
2. GENDER: No main effect, but significant interaction with age
3. INTENT MATTERS: Different usage intents predict different anthropomorphization levels
4. INTENT x AGE: Intent is significantly associated with age (p=0.0006)
5. THE STORY: Teen males, especially those creating characters, are the
   highest anthropomorphizers

================================================================================
CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. TEENS ANTHROPOMORPHIZE MORE: Robust finding across multiple analyses.
   Effect is small (d=0.11) but highly significant (p<0.0001).

2. GENDER EFFECT IS WEAK: No main effect of gender. Unexpected finding
   that high anthropomorphizers are MORE likely to be male.

3. INTENT MATTERS: Users engaged in character creation show highest
   anthropomorphization levels. This may explain some of the age effect
   (teens prefer character creation), but age effect persists within intents.

4. THE TYPICAL HIGH ANTHROPOMORPHIZER: Teen male engaged in character
   creation, actively participating in discussions.
