STUDY CONTEXT
----------------------------------------
  Observed effect size (f²): 0.0007
  Interpretation: negligible

  Power to detect observed effect: 29.5%

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------
  The observed effect (f² ≈ 0.0007) is far below even 'small' effects (f² = 0.02).
  However, measurement error (46.3% accuracy) reduces power substantially.

  Key conclusions:
  1. With 27,000 observations and 46.3% reliability,
     we can only detect true effects ≥ f² = 0.0022 at 80% power
  2. A 'small' effect (f² = 0.02) would be detected with >99% power even with error
  3. The null result likely reflects genuinely negligible effects,
     not effects masked by measurement error

  Recommendation: Frame as 'effects too small to matter' rather than
  'no effects exist' - we cannot rule out very tiny effects
