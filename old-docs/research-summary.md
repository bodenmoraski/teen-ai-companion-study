# The Illusion Project: Research Summary

## Anthropomorphization of AI Companions

**January 2026 | Final Findings**

---

## What We Studied

We analyzed **47,062 Reddit users** who discuss AI companion apps (like Character.AI and Replika) to understand who treats AI as human-like and what factors predict this behavior.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Who Uses AI Companions?

| Demographic | Finding |
|-------------|---------|
| **Gender** | 79% male, 21% female |
| **Age** | ~52% teen (13-18), ~48% adult (19+) |

The user base is predominantly male. Age distribution is more balanced than previously thought.

---

### 2. Does Gender Predict Anthropomorphization?

**Yes, but the effect is small.**

- **Females anthropomorphize AI companions more than males**
- Effect size: d = -0.11 (small)
- Females are 33% more likely to be high anthropomorphizers (OR = 1.33)
- This holds across different analysis approaches

---

### 3. Does Age Predict Anthropomorphization?

**No—and here's where it gets interesting.**

Using our predictive models (95% accuracy):
- **No meaningful difference** between predicted teens and adults
- Effect size: d = 0.01 (negligible)

But using verified self-declared ages (n = 286):
- **Adults actually anthropomorphize MORE than teens**
- Effect size: d = -0.30 (small)
- This is the opposite of what many assume!

**Bottom line:** The common assumption that "digital native" teens are more likely to treat AI as human-like is not supported. If anything, actual adults do this more.

---

### 4. What About Emotional Expression?

High anthropomorphizers show distinctive emotional patterns:

| More of... | Less of... |
|------------|------------|
| Anger | Neutrality |
| Fear | Sadness |
| Disgust | - |

This suggests anthropomorphization is linked to **emotional activation**, not passive engagement. Users who treat AI as human-like express stronger emotions when discussing their AI companions.

---

### 5. How Much Do Demographics Really Matter?

**Very little.**

Demographics (age + gender combined) explain less than 1% of the variation in anthropomorphization. This means:

- Individual psychological factors likely matter much more
- Targeting users by demographics for AI safety purposes may not be effective
- Future research should focus on personality, attachment style, and AI design features

---

## Limitations

- Reddit-only sample (may not generalize to other platforms)
- Correlational design (can't prove causation)
- Effect sizes are small throughout
- Self-declared age sample is limited (n = 286)

---

## Implications

### For AI Companies
- Gender matters more than age for user experience design
- Don't assume young users need more "protection" from anthropomorphization

### For AI Safety
- High anthropomorphizers show more emotionally activated discussion patterns
- This may indicate deeper psychological investment worth monitoring

### For Researchers
- Behavioral age classifiers may capture "acting young" rather than "being young"
- Always validate with ground truth when possible

---

## The Bottom Line

**AI companion users who anthropomorphize more are more likely to be female and express more intense emotions—but demographics explain almost nothing about who treats AI as human-like.** The search for what drives anthropomorphization should look beyond simple demographics toward individual psychology and AI design.
